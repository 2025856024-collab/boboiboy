Papa Zola is a self-proclaimed superhero and school teacher in BoBoiBoy who fights for justice and truth. Known for his dramatic flair and catchphrases, he brings comedic wisdom and mentorship to the heroes.

MORE INFO

Papa Zola is an energetic, dramatic, and comedic superhero in the BoBoiBoy series who is famously known for his over-the-top, constant preaching about "justice" and "truth." Although he views himself as a legendary figure, his exaggerated personality and complete lack of self-awareness often make him a primary source of humor in the franchise. He frequently turns simple, everyday situations into complicated, chaotic, and funny scenarios because of his eccentric behavior and inflated sense of importance. Despite his quirks, he remains a beloved and iconic character who provides a unique blend of heart and comedy to the show's narrative. His enthusiastic voice is brought to life by Nizam Razak, who is the co-founder of Animonsta Studios and the creative visionary behind the entire BoBoiBoy franchise. By balancing his role as a self-proclaimed protector with his unpredictable and often hilarious antics, Papa Zola has cemented his place as an essential, entertaining, and memorable staple of the series, ensuring that every appearance he makes is filled with action, drama, and endless laughter for fans of all ages.

VOICE ACTOR

Nizam Razak is a multi-talented figure in the Malaysian animation industry, best known for his pivotal role as the co-founder and Managing Director of Animonsta Studios. While he is the creative mastermind behind the BoBoiBoy franchise, directing and writing its world-building narratives, he also serves as the energetic voice behind the iconic character Papa Zola.

His work as a voice actor extends beyond the legendary superhero teacher, as he has lent his voice to several other characters in both the Bahasa Malaysia and English versions of the series, including Bago Go, Wak Baga Ga, and the Burgerman. His contribution to the franchise is deep-rooted, as he oversees the creative vision that brought characters like Adu Du and Papa Zola to life. It is also worth noting that in certain instances, specifically for the character's signature intense screaming, Nurasyikin Abdul Khalid has provided the additional vocal performance for Papa Zola. Through these roles, Nizam Razak has helped establish Monsta as a significant force in producing high-quality animated content that resonates with both local and international audiences.
