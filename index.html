Monsta Studios Sdn. Bhd. (formerly Animonsta Studios) is a leading Malaysian animation company founded on September 15, 2009, by four former Les' Copaque Production staff members: Nizam Razak, Anas Abdul Aziz, Safwan Abd Karim, and Kee Yong Pin. Headquartered in Cyberjaya, the co-founders leveraged their creative experience to build an independent studio dedicated to producing high-quality, action-packed 3D animated content for both local and global markets.

The studio achieved massive commercial success with its flagship franchise, BoBoiBoy, which evolved from a hit television series into a regional phenomenon with two record-breaking feature films. Monsta has since expanded its creative footprint into a shared cinematic continuity known as the Power Sphera Universe, which includes the globally acclaimed prequel series Mechamato. Today, the company has grown into a highly diversified media empire, expanding its intellectual properties through extensive merchandising, publishing, and interactive digital experiences on platforms like Roblox.
