BOBOIBOY

BoBoiBoy is a young superhero with elemental powers who fights alongside his friends to protect Earth from alien threats and villains seeking Power Spheres.

MORE INFO

BoBoiBoy is an iconic Malaysian animated franchise that follows a young, kind-hearted student who gains extraordinary elemental superpowers after encountering a sentient power sphere named Ochobot. As he discovers his ability to control elements including lightning, wind, fire, earth, and water. The transitions from an ordinary schoolboy into a brave defender of the galaxy.

He is rarely alone in his mission ,he is supported by a loyal team of friends, including Yaya, Ying, Gopal, and Fang, each possessing their own unique skills. Together, they balance the everyday challenges of adolescence with the high-stakes responsibility of protecting Earth from various intergalactic threats and power-hungry villains like Adu Du.

The series is widely celebrated for its high-octane action sequences, dynamic character development, and vibrant animation style. Beyond the spectacle, it emphasizes essential values such as teamwork, courage, perseverance, and the enduring strength of friendship. Since its inception, the franchise has evolved into a major cultural phenomenon across Southeast Asia. Through multiple television seasons, the BoBoiBoy Galaxy series, and successful feature films, the brand has set a high benchmark for regional animation, successfully blending modern science-fiction tropes with relatable, local sensibilities that resonate with audiences of all ages worldwide.

VOICE ACTOR

Nur Fathiah binti Diaz is a celebrated Malaysian voice actress who has become a household name as the definitive voice of the protagonist in the BoBoiBoy franchise since its inception in 2011. Starting her career at a young age, she first rose to prominence as the original voice of the twins in Upin & Ipin before transitioning to Animonsta Studios to bring BoBoiBoy's brave and youthful persona to life across multiple series and blockbuster films. Her work is a family affair, as her younger brother Muhammad Fathi Diaz voices the character's robotic companion, Ochobot, creating a unique off-screen bond that mirrors the show's core themes. Beyond the recording booth, she is a graduate who has balanced her iconic status in Southeast Asian animation with professional roles in education and personal milestones, including her marriage in late 2023, while remaining a beloved figure for fans throughout the region.

BOBOIBOY ELEMENTAL SPLIT

Elemental Split is BoBoiBoy's ultimate power, allowing him to divide into three, five, or seven separate bodies, each wielding a unique elemental strength. While this allows him to battle multiple foes simultaneously, it carries the risk of memory loss and exhaustion if used for too long. Each split personality reflects its specific element, and this ability serves as the essential foundation for his most powerful Elemental Fusions.

BOBOIBOY

BoBoiBoy Lightning is the high-speed elemental form of BoBoiBoy, embodying raw kinetic energy and intense power. Defined by his signature yellow color scheme and lightning-bolt motifs, this form grants him incredible velocity, allowing him to overwhelm opponents with lightning-fast strikes that are nearly impossible to track.

His personality in this form is notably impulsive and short-tempered, reflecting a desire to resolve conflicts instantly. While his rapid-fire agility is a major asset in combat, his tendency to favor speed over strategy can occasionally be a liability. Nevertheless, he remains a vital, aggressive offensive force in every battle.
