YING

Ying is a cheerful and energetic member of the team who possesses the power to manipulate time, allowing her to move at incredible speeds. She is a vital ally who uses her speed to support her friends during battles.

MORE INFO

Ying is a dynamic and essential member of the team, widely celebrated for her cheerful personality and extraordinary time-manipulation abilities. Her core power, which allows her to control time, grants her the incredible capability to move at super-speeds, making her one of the most agile and versatile fighters in the group. This power is not merely for offense; she frequently utilizes her speed to swiftly navigate complex battlefields, provide critical support to her allies, and execute strategic maneuvers that often turn the tide of a conflict.

Beyond her tactical utility, Ying is recognized for her unwavering loyalty and optimistic spirit. Even in the most challenging situations, she remains a pillar of moral support for her friends, often using her speed to protect those in danger. Her character arc highlights a strong commitment to self-improvement and teamwork, as she continuously balances her academic responsibilities with her role as a hero. Ying’s energetic approach to challenges and her ability to think quickly on her feet make her a fan-favorite character. Ultimately, her blend of speed, sharp reflexes, and compassionate nature cements her reputation as a cornerstone of the team, proving that her impact on the series is defined as much by her kind heart as it is by her legendary speed.

VOICE ACTOR

Ying is brought to life by a diverse group of talented voice actors, each contributing unique nuances to her energetic and cheerful persona across various languages and series iterations. In the original Malay version, the role is famously performed by Yap Ee Jean, whose portrayal helped establish the character's core identity as the team's optimistic and fast-moving speedster.

For international English-speaking audiences, the character has been voiced by different performers depending on the production. Chan Su Ling provided the voice for the earlier Disney and YouTube releases, while Starr Leavitt took over the role to bring a fresh perspective to the character for BoBoiBoy Galaxy Season 2. Beyond these versions, Ying has also been localized for global fans through distinct performances by actors such as Mace for the Chinese dub, Jessy Milianty for the Indonesian version, and the renowned Aoi Yuuki for the Japanese release. Each of these actors plays a critical role in maintaining the emotional consistency and high-energy spirit of the character, ensuring that Ying remains a relatable and beloved icon for fans worldwide as she balances her superhero duties with her student life.
